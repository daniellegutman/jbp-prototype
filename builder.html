Job-Based Performance PlanNEW
Reward crew members based on job-by-job eligibility — payout tied to who was on that job.
How it works: ShareWillow evaluates each job against your eligibility criteria. Qualifying jobs generate an award paid to enrolled crew members who were on that job.

Select the type of award

Choose how job-based bonuses are structured for this plan, then set the compensation model.

Single Role By Job
Each enrolled person earns their own award per eligible job. Best when one role is responsible per job — e.g. Crew Lead, Project Manager, Primary Tech.
Compensation Model
Multi-Role by Job
Each eligible job creates a shared pool divided among the people who worked it. Best for plans with multiple people per job — e.g. Crew Members, Multiple Assigned Technicians.

How is the job pool split?

NEW

Define how each eligible job's award pool is divided among the enrolled crew members who were on that job.

Equal share among all crew on the job
Pool divided evenly regardless of hours or role.
By percentage of time on job
Each crew member's share is proportional to their hours on the job. Requires timesheet data.
Example: Pool = $134.40 · Carlos 4.5h of 9h total = 50% → $67.20 · Derek 4.5h = 50% → $67.20
